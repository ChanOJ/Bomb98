  ZAPPY
[PC code]
Name : TERDIMAN Pierre
Email : terdiman_p@???? or zappy@????

He started coding years ago on APPLE IIe, then on AMSTRAD CPC 464. He felt in love with demos on ATARI 520 STF, started asm coding in 1989 the day when he had his brain cut to pieces by the CareBears' Cuddly Demos... He joined the well-known ST crew called HOLOCAUST, did some intros, screens, gfxs, as well as complete demos still vividly remembered nowadays as some of the most technical ones ever coded on this machine. After his last demo on ST (BLOOD coded with ELRIC, who really was the only other member of HOLOCAUST) he left the ST scene for the PC. He formed IGLOO with PATAPOM and FLAN, and released some code in a demo called FLIRT (4th at the SATURNE party 3 in April 1996). He joined BOMB in the middle of this year, as a TRAUMA coder! Zappy has a titanic crush on BJORK, he likes to code in pure assembler, weird and pervert coding ("THERE'S NO CODING LIKE DEMO CODING!" - Digital Insanity in MINDBOMB), writing novels or gig reviews, singing stupid cartoons' tunes as well as Jeff Buckley's songs, Iceland, Zelazny/Adams/Zimmer Bradley/Vance, and he loves - oh Lord, yes! - CAPPUCCINO!!!!
