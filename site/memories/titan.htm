  TITAN
[BITMAP GFX, 3D & MUSIC]
Name : SEBIRE Laurent

Titan began scene activities on C64, where he drawn his very first graphics. He bought in 1990 his first AMIGA, and joined the well-known demogroup called ANARCHY. He did some contributions in COMPLEX and DIGITAL demos. He joined BOMB in 1995, drew in SHAFT 7 and worked on BOMB [with IMPACT STUDIO] and EXPLORA, who was GENGIS last PC demo. He loves: working on Lightwave, doing musics and burning cds...
