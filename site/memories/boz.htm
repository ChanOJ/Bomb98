Name : TARTELIN Stephane

Started up demo-scene activities drawing on Amstrad CPC in the end of '80, with the wellknown OCP art studio :). Few months later he bought an Amiga 500 and tasted the Deluxe Paint power, in the same time he started modeling with "VERTEX 3D" & "IMAGINE", the first POLYGONAL experience... He joined Arkham and brought a PC, selling his old Amiga. Now he's using TV paint & 3DSmax for creating fantastic & funny stylized ANIMALS. A common passion for Pandas & Penguins with Made, strange, isn't it? maybe something from their childhood. February 1998, he joined BOMB. Nowadays he works for game company and studies Fine Arts. These favorits bands are: Deftones, KoRn, Pennywise, LimpBizkit, Mindjive, Incubus, millencolin...
