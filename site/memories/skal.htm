In 1986, after a hard stuggle with its parent, he eventually succeeded in having them buy him a...C64!! Marvellous world of game-cracking, 650x ASM-coding, and sleepless nights opened wide to its bewildered eyes. And life went on cracking, coding, etc... until 1989, when its studies requirements overwhelmed its eagerness for real-time raytraced 3-dimensionnal mandelbrot set on its beloved C64 (really?!:). Later on, in 1992, he was off with its studies, and having time to get back to computer, which had massively evolved since the pionneering times of C64 & CPC... Successively coding an Sparcs, HP, Cray and PC, he ended trying some realtime 3D on its brand new P120, hence making its 10 year old dream come true.
