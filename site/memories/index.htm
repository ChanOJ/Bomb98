BOMB story began in a warm summer of 1994, after ORIGIN [1st at the PARTY 3-AMIGA demo] and R.E.A.L [1st at SATURNE party 2-AMIGA demo], when GENGIS decided to leave COMPLEX in order to found his own group with CLAWZ and HOF. CASUAL was the first production, presented at the 3S party held on the 02-04 september 1994 [1st AMIGA 40k]. December 1994, MOTION was presented at the PARTY 4 [3th AMIGA demo] held in Denemark featuring a lot of new effects and mostly the beginning of FEARS' 3D engine... the first DOOM-like on AMIGA. TITAN joined. After a long year of non-stop coding Gengis decided to have a break and signed up with MANYK, a game company, to develop FEARS on Amiga 1200 and CD32. January 1996, MADE joined the team. Ten months later the game was finished & sold in Germany & UK. Meanwhile, in mars the future BOMB PC released a demo under IMPACT STUDIO's label strangeously called BOMB ;), which finished 2nd at the SATURNE party 3. November, after 4 days of hard-work, EXPLORA was presented at the SATURNE party 4 [2nd PC demo entry], with the first codes of GENGIS's powerful 3d engine. Short time after, ZAPPY, PATAPOM [ex-IGLOO], BEN and AXEL [ex-MTP] joined BOMB forces and CLAWZ left for OXYGENE. BEN & AXEL won AMIGA demo compo with SHAFT 7 at PARTY 6, and we all spent a very happy new year! February 1997, ZAPPY & PATAPOM's SHIANLEE demo won the VOLCANIC party 3...A new bomberman increase BOMB forces, October 8th 1997 SKAL joined as PC coder and Linux specialist... Nowadays GENGIS lives in London working for a game company, CLAWZ studies, TITAN works in Hospital, MADE is working for a game company, ZAPPY studies computing, PATAPOM sleeps, SKAL cooks des croquettes de poissons, AXEL works and BEN is at school :). Stay tuned for our forthcoming productions!
